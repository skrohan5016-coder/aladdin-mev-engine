- exact accepted F4 parent identity;
- stateless direct-runtime executor interface and deployment specifications that bind one exact recorded flash-loan provider/source, `msg.value` direct-payment semantics, `msg.sender` as the complete base-token residual beneficiary, and the canonical empty storage root;
- exact F2 deployment proof binding, exact deployment-registry membership, and one-code-hash/one-interface authority;
- route-command bytes derived only from the exact F4 execution plan, with funding kind/provider/source matching the authenticated executor interface;
- canonical ABI calldata with a fail-closed public encoder that validates the exact route frame, unique pools, one simple base-token cycle, principal and amount continuity, nonzero identities, positive flash principal, an exact final command minimum, uint64 deadline, an exact millisecond validity ceiling that cannot expand through second rounding, and ceiling-rounded route/final slippage minima that preserve F4 economics;
- authenticated EOA sender nonce and balance sharing the exact state anchor;
- canonical unsigned EIP-1559 type-2 RLP preimage and signing hash with a canonical intrinsic-gas floor and authenticated sender-balance coverage for explicit L1-data, OP Stack operator-fee, and direct-payment upper bounds;
- authenticated-anchor-fresh, bounded target-block private bundle intent beginning at the authenticated nonce, with contiguous nonces, complete-bundle balance coverage, and no relay data;
- transaction simulations bound to the exact anchor, transaction, signing hash, bundle, explicit in-range block number, a block timestamp strictly after the authenticated anchor and no later than the executor deadline, and max-fee-covered base fee, with gas no lower than intrinsic and no higher than the signed gas limit, operator fee no higher than its recorded upper bound, observed no earlier than that intent, and reconciling the exact F4 base-token route output, principal repayment, flash fee, pre-external-cost residual, and complete residual delivery to the authenticated sender;
- final package-v1 recomputation for exactly one bound unsigned transaction, with deadline-transitive validity and all signing, submission, execution, and inclusion authority disabled;
- twelve closed F5 schemas and their canonical schema lock.

## Required CI

The exact source head and current synthetic merge independently pass compilation, the complete unit/adversarial suite, repository policy, architecture lock, source-contract lock, F2/F3/F4/F5 schema locks, and a non-mutating clean-worktree check with read-only permissions and immutable action pins.
